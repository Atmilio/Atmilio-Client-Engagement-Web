Know where AI can
make a difference.

Understand how your team works today, where AI may help, and what it could take to put improvements into practice.

A PRACTICAL BASELINE

Your team's AI efficiency score

We assess workflows and AI readiness across four areas. The proposed 0–100 score assigns up to 25 points to each area, with the evidence and gaps behind each rating documented.

Workflow opportunityData and toolsTeam readinessControls and oversight

The score is an assessment framework, not a guarantee of savings or a measure of employee performance. Criteria and weighting will be agreed for each engagement.

From insight to an action plan

01
Baseline and score

Review selected tasks, time spent, current tools, adoption, and safeguards. Identify where information is missing.

02
Opportunities to improve

Highlight and prioritize candidate workflows by potential impact, feasibility, and risk, with a recommended next step for each.

03
Costs and potential return

Estimate implementation, subscriptions, integration, training, and ongoing review costs alongside a range of possible benefits.

How we estimate return

For each opportunity, estimated net benefit is the value of benefits realized minus implementation and operating costs for the period. Estimated ROI is net benefit divided by those costs, expressed as a percentage when costs are greater than zero. Estimates depend on your baseline, assumptions, adoption, and results; they are not guaranteed.
